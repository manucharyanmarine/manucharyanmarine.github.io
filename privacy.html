Privacy Policy

HarMar Games — applies to the game LampBloom (Android).
Effective date: 23 November 2026

Summary

LampBloom does not ask for your name, email or any account. Your game progress stays on your device. Ads are optional: they appear only when you choose to watch one (for a hint or bonus stars). Our ad partner, Unity LevelPlay, receives device data to deliver those ads, as described below.

Data the game keeps on your device

Level progress, stars, the daily-puzzle streak, collection choices, awards and settings (sound, music, vibration, language, ad personalisation) are stored only on your device. We do not receive or store them on our servers. Uninstalling the game deletes this data.

Advertising

LampBloom shows optional rewarded ads through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies, including ironSource Ads and Unity Ads). When the ad service is used, it may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, language and country, and information about the ads you see or interact with. It uses this data to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud.

On first launch the game asks whether ads may be personalised using your advertising ID. If you choose “No”, you can still watch ads, but they are not based on your interests. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Personalized ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

How Unity handles this data is described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, which also explains how to exercise your rights with Unity.

Children

LampBloom is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU/EEA or the UK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to withdraw consent. Because we do not store personal data ourselves, most requests concern the ad partner; you can withdraw consent in the game's settings and contact Unity through the policy linked above, or contact us and we will help.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes. The effective date above shows the latest version.
